# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Minimal world

Build a small deterministic 2D world with five inhabitants. Include a grid, food, water, fatigue, movement, eating, drinking, resting, speech, bounded perception, memories, imperfect beliefs, death, event logging, snapshots, inspection, and replay. Begin with scripted decision controllers, then connect the local model.

Success means a run can be reproduced, hidden information stays hidden, resource consumption is valid, and an observer can explain an individual decision using only information available to that inhabitant.

Run the first milestone across multiple seeds and include a minimal browser map, inhabitant inspector, event timeline, and replay controls.

## Phase 2 — Material survival

Add gathering, exposure and shelter alternatives, material properties, crafting experiments, tools, construction, destruction, hazards, injuries, relationships, and basic planning support. Preserve the same authority boundary and provenance rules.

Success means useful techniques can be discovered or lost without a recipe registry being exposed to inhabitants, while experiments account for knowledge already present in the pretrained model.

## Phase 3 — Generational and social continuity

Add reproduction, inherited traits, development, imitation, teaching, trade, groups, durable artifacts, and repeated social practices. Analyze whether stable roles, institutions, norms, or inequalities appear without being named in the inhabitant interface.

Success means genetic inheritance and cultural transmission can be separated in experiments.

## Phase 4 — Rich ecology and experimentation

Add predators or competitors, disease, climate, seasons, renewable and depleting resources, scenario design, branching timelines, automated experiment batches, and model/controller comparisons.

Success means hypotheses about emergence can be tested across seeds and conditions rather than inferred from a memorable single run.
